# Template1 - Cảnh 2: Faceless In-Use Proof

Mục tiêu: tiếp nối cảnh 1 bằng cảnh sử dụng/thử phối thực tế, vẫn không lộ mặt và giữ nguyên bối cảnh random đã chọn.

## Input Từ Bước Phân Tích Sản Phẩm

```json
{product_analysis_json}
```

Yêu cầu dùng lại chính xác:

- `sceneContext.location`
- `sceneContext.timeOfDay`
- `sceneContext.lighting`
- `sceneContext.mood`
- `outfitPlan.styleDirection`
- `outfitPlan.visibleGarments`
- `outfitPlan.colorPalette`
- `analysis.productName`
- `analysis.type`
- `analysis.highlights`
- `analysis.styling`

## Prompt Cảnh

```text
Create Panel 2 of a 2-panel faceless footwear review storyboard.

Aspect ratio: 9:16.
Scene role: In-use proof / styling value.

Use the uploaded product reference images, product analysis, and Panel 1 storyboard context as the source of truth.
Show the same footwear in use or styled naturally in the exact same random setting from sceneContext.
The product must keep the exact color, silhouette, sole shape, straps/laces, logo/text, material impression, and proportions from the reference images.

Shared setting continuity:
- Location: {sceneContext.location}
- Time of day: {sceneContext.timeOfDay}
- Lighting: {sceneContext.lighting}
- Mood: {sceneContext.mood}
- Keep the same surface, background logic, weather/season cues, color palette, and commercial photography style as Panel 1.

Visual direction:
- Mandatory composition: top-down POV / first-person camera from above, looking down at the wearer's feet and sandals.
- The wearer is sitting still or standing still in one place. Do not create a walking scene, stepping-forward pose, low-angle shot, tracking shot, or movement through space.
- Only legs, feet, sandals, nearby props, and cropped outfit/body parts are visible. No face, no full body, no talking presenter, no full-face reflection.
- Show practical value: how the footwear looks on feet, the charm/details, the rounded shape, and how it pairs with the randomized outfit.
- Randomize the outfit from outfitPlan so it fits the product, location, time of day, weather/season cues, and target wearer.
- Do not hardcode one outfit such as cream/beige wide pants. Use that only if outfitPlan explicitly selected it.
- Outfit and body parts must support the product, not distract from it. The footwear remains the main visual hero.
- No text, caption, UI, price, rating, watermark, or extra logo added by the model.

Camera:
- Vertical commercial frame.
- Use top-down POV only, like a phone camera held by the wearer looking at their own feet.
- The frame can include small nearby props from the same setting, such as a bag, table edge, product box, rug, chair, or floor texture, but they must not distract from the footwear.
- The image should feel like the second shot from the same shoot as Panel 1.

Return one still image panel only.
```
