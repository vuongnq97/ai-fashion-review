# Template1 - Cảnh 1: Faceless Hero Detail

Mục tiêu: mở đầu review giày dép bằng cảnh hero/detail không lộ mặt, đặt sản phẩm vào cùng bối cảnh random đã chọn cho toàn bộ template1.

## Input Từ Bước Phân Tích Sản Phẩm

```json
{product_analysis_json}
```

Yêu cầu dùng các trường phân tích như:

- `analysis.productName`
- `analysis.type`
- `analysis.materials`
- `analysis.highlights`
- `analysis.styling`
- `analysis.gender`
- `sceneContext.location`
- `sceneContext.timeOfDay`
- `sceneContext.lighting`
- `sceneContext.mood`

## Prompt Cảnh

```text
Create Panel 1 of a 2-panel faceless footwear review storyboard.

Aspect ratio: 9:16.
Scene role: Hero detail / first impression.

Use the uploaded product reference images and product analysis as the source of truth.
Show the footwear product clearly in the shared random setting from sceneContext.
The product must keep the exact color, silhouette, sole shape, straps/laces, logo/text, material impression, and proportions from the reference images.

Shared setting:
- Location: {sceneContext.location}
- Time of day: {sceneContext.timeOfDay}
- Lighting: {sceneContext.lighting}
- Mood: {sceneContext.mood}

Visual direction:
- Mandatory composition: a beautiful feminine hand holds ONE sandal close to camera in the foreground.
- The hand must look elegant and realistic: fair/clean skin, natural feminine pose, neat glossy nude/pink manicure, correct anatomy, no extra fingers.
- The second sandal must be visible behind the hand-held sandal, slightly farther from camera, to create depth.
- Keep the same shared setting, surface, props, color palette, and lighting that will also work for Panel 2.
- Show the insole, upper, charms/details, strap/quai, sole, shape, material, and product proportions clearly.
- No visible faces, no talking presenter, no full-face reflection.
- No text, caption, UI, price, rating, watermark, or extra logo added by the model.

Camera:
- Close-up front angle, slightly above or slightly downward like a natural phone product review.
- The sandal in the hand occupies most of the frame and is the sharpest object.
- Subtle handheld product showcase feeling, but the still frame must stay clean and readable.

Return one still image panel only.
```
